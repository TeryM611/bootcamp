The 4-week map

Each week is one session: about 30 minutes of taught content, a live walkthrough together, then guided practice in TryHackMe rooms. Momentum compounds — every week builds directly on the last.

Run the session

Pick a week to open its slide deck and the TryHackMe rooms assigned for practice. Use Present for fullscreen, and the ← / → arrow keys to move through slides.
